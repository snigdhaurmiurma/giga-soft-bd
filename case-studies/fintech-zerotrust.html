Multi-Cloud Migration & Zero-Trust Banking Core

Migrated mission-critical financial workloads off aging single-site hardware into a multi-region Zero-Trust Kubernetes architecture with Istio mutual TLS 1.3 micro-segmentation, automated failover, and continuous VAPT compliance telemetry.

Executive Overview & Business Context

A regulated financial services provider handling hundreds of thousands of daily payment settlements operated on aging on-premise server racks with a flat internal network perimeter. Rising hardware maintenance costs, peak-hour settlement slowdowns, and strict regulatory audit requirements demanded a modern, resilient Zero-Trust cloud foundation.

The Operational & Technical Challenge

Traditional perimeter firewalls created a high-risk blast radius: once inside the corporate network, internal services communicated over unencrypted channels without granular service identity verification. Meanwhile, manual failover procedures during power or ISP disruptions threatened regulatory SLA thresholds.

  • Single-site hardware bottlenecks causing settlement latency spikes during salary-week and festival transaction surges.
  • Flat internal network topology without workload-level identity or east-west encryption.
  • High recurring capital expenditure on underutilized physical servers provisioned only for peak loads.
  • Time-intensive manual evidence collection for annual regulatory cybersecurity and VAPT audits.

Solution Architecture & Engineering Execution

Giga Soft Limited architected an Infrastructure-as-Code multi-region Kubernetes platform using Terraform, Istio Service Mesh, and HashiCorp Vault. Every microservice communicates exclusively via mutually authenticated TLS 1.3 certificates rotated automatically, backed by active-active database replication and continuous vulnerability scanning in CI/CD.

  • Strict Zero-Trust Network Access (ZTNA) with Istio mTLS 1.3 micro-segmentation across every payment and ledger pod.
  • Automated secrets management, dynamic database credentials, and HSM-backed encryption keys via HashiCorp Vault.
  • Active-active multi-zone failover with automated horizontal pod autoscaling eliminating peak-hour degradation.
  • Continuous SIEM telemetry, immutable audit logging, and automated OWASP/VAPT compliance gates before every release.

Measured Production Outcomes

Following blue-green production cutover with zero customer-facing downtime, the platform achieved 99.999% availability across consecutive peak settlement cycles while reducing total infrastructure spend by 42% through right-sized autoscaling. Independent third-party VAPT testing confirmed zero critical or high-severity vulnerabilities.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Zero-Trust architecture essential for FinTech and banking platforms?

Zero-Trust eliminates implicit trust inside the internal network by authenticating and encrypting every service-to-service and user-to-API request, dramatically reducing breach impact and simplifying ISO 27001 and regulatory compliance.

How did multi-cloud migration reduce infrastructure cost by 42%?

Replacing over-provisioned static servers with Terraform-managed autoscaling node pools, spot/preemptible batch workers, and tiered storage lifecycle policies aligned compute spend directly to real transaction demand.

Does Giga Soft provide ongoing 24/7 security monitoring and VAPT audits?

Yes. Our Cyber Security and DevOps teams provide continuous SIEM alerting, quarterly penetration testing, patch governance, and 24/7 Tier-3 incident response SLAs.
