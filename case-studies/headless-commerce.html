High-Scale Headless E-Commerce & bKash Engine

Architected a decoupled Next.js edge storefront paired with Redis catalog caching, sub-second faceted product filtering, asynchronous bKash/Nagad/SSLCommerz payment queues, and 98/100 technical SEO scores.

Executive Overview & Business Context

A leading multi-category retail brand in Bangladesh faced recurring revenue loss during Eid, Pohela Boishakh, and flash-sale campaigns when its legacy monolithic PHP storefront buckled under concurrent mobile shoppers. Slow 4.5-second page loads on 4G mobile networks depressed both organic Google rankings and checkout completion rates.

The Operational & Technical Challenge

Every category filter and cart update hit the primary relational database synchronously, while payment gateway webhook timeouts from bKash and card processors occasionally created duplicate orders or stuck inventory holds during high-concurrency promotions.

  • 4.5s+ mobile Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) causing high bounce rates and weak Core Web Vitals.
  • Storefront crashes and database exhaustion during 5x–10x flash-sale traffic bursts.
  • Checkout abandonment triggered by slow payment redirects and webhook reconciliation race conditions.
  • Fragmented inventory sync between online orders, flagship retail POS counters, and courier partners.

Solution Architecture & Engineering Execution

Giga Soft Limited decoupled the commerce stack into a server-rendered Next.js frontend backed by a GraphQL API gateway, Redis in-memory catalog cache, and Kafka-backed idempotent checkout queues. Product pages are statically and incrementally rendered at the edge with rich Product, Offer, and Breadcrumb structured data for Google Shopping and organic search.

  • Decoupled Next.js SSR/ISR storefront achieving 0.48s average load time and 98/100 Google Lighthouse SEO score.
  • Idempotent bKash, Nagad, and SSLCommerz payment processing with automated webhook reconciliation and retry queues.
  • Sub-50ms Redis-powered catalog search, size/color faceted filtering, and real-time stock reservation.
  • Automated Steadfast, Pathao, and RedX courier booking with live parcel tracking and SMS/WhatsApp notifications.

Measured Production Outcomes

During its very first Eid campaign on the new headless architecture, the merchant processed record concurrent orders with zero downtime, increased Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) by 210% year-over-year, and saw organic non-branded search traffic climb sharply thanks to sub-second Core Web Vitals and structured product schema.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is headless Next.js better than a standard template store for high-traffic retail in Bangladesh?

Headless architecture decouples storefront speed from heavy backend database queries, enabling sub-second 4G mobile loads, zero flash-sale crashes, full custom checkout UX, and top Google Core Web Vitals rankings.

Which local payment gateways and couriers can Giga Soft integrate?

We natively integrate bKash, Nagad, Rocket, SSLCommerz, AmarPay, Stripe, and courier APIs including Steadfast, Pathao, RedX, and Paperfly with automated reconciliation.

Does the headless storefront include technical SEO optimization out of the box?

Yes. Every storefront ships with server-side rendering, clean semantic URLs, dynamic XML sitemaps, canonical tags, Open Graph metadata, and Product/Review Schema.org JSON-LD.
