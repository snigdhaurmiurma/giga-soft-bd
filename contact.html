Data Protection & Confidentiality

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 | Giga Soft Limited

At Giga Soft Limited ("Giga Soft", "we", "our", or "us"), we prioritize the security, confidentiality, and integrity of your corporate and personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines our data governance policies, collection mechanisms, and your legal rights in accordance with international data security standards and the Information and Communication Technology (ICT) Act of Bangladesh.

1. Scope & Information We Collect

We only collect data necessary to provide high-quality technology advisory, custom software engineering, and customer support. This includes:

  • Client Contact Details: Name, professional title, corporate email address, and phone number when requesting proposals or consultations.
  • Project Briefs & Technical Specifications: Data, API specifications, and workflow documentation submitted for project estimation and execution.
  • Technical Analytics: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic telemetry (browser type, anonymized IP addresses, session duration) used strictly to optimize website performance.

2. Zero Third-Party Selling Guarantee

Giga Soft maintains an uncompromising stance on data monetization. We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal or enterprise data to data brokers, advertising networks, or third parties under any circumstances.

3. Enterprise Data Security & Encryption

All data transmitted through our website and client management systems is protected by Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) with 256-bit encryption. Stored project documentation is housed in hardened cloud environments featuring role-based Zero-Trust access control and automated off-site backups.

4. Confidentiality & Mutual Non-Disclosure

Every software development project, architectural consultation, and proprietary business idea entrusted to Giga Soft is automatically protected under strict mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) standards.

5. Inquiries & Data Rights

You have the right to request access to, rectification of, or deletion of your stored contact records. Inquiries regarding our data protection protocols can be directed to our compliance team at gigasoftbd@gmail.com.
